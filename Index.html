1 · Week and days
Relaxed: each SKU's Lines column, and the category home-line preference. Those record current practice, which is the thing being tested — hold them fixed and the answer can only be "what you already do".
Never relaxed: allergen chain, sausage/sesame separation, USDA approval, capper and small-cup equipment, night restrictions, slot cap. Line seeds stay too — a seed is a low-allergen chain starter, not a line-assignment policy.
Both passes run with no curfew, so they balance on even hours and the comparison isn't distorted by start-time weighting.
2 · What should the new line be?
Line to add: runs on:
Tries every combination of start time and capabilities for that line, solving the days you ticked above through the real engine each time, and ranks them. Every SKU is made admissible on it for the search — capabilities still gate what can actually land — so whatever the engine puts there is the proposed SKU list. Nothing is changed and nothing goes live; this is a simulation.
Any candidate that produces an allergen chain break is disqualified outright, not ranked lower. Ranked by unplaced, then overtime, then latest finish, then churn — a config that moves five SKUs for most of the gain beats one that moves thirty.
It cannot recommend headcount: runtime is qty ÷ upm and upm is a per-SKU constant, so every candidate would say the same thing.
